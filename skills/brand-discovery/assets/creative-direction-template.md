# Creative direction template

Copy to `creative-direction.md` at the project root. This is a **starter, not an approved direction**.

- Business facts (offer, buyer, money action, proof available and missing) live in `PRD.md` and `media-inventory.md`. Point to them; don't copy them here.
- Keep confirmed, observed, guessed and unknown apart. A guess stays labeled until the owner confirms it.
- Questionnaires, returned PDFs and raw answers stay in the project's gitignored `discovery/` folder. This file records what they decided.
