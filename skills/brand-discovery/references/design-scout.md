# Design scout: references and the template search brief

The builder chooses the template. Many come from subscriptions the builder already has (Envato, MotionSites), so trying one costs nothing. The scout's job is to make that search short: say what kind of design this business needs, show a few sites that do it well, and hand over the filters and search words that get close.

It runs after the market read and is written into `market-read.md` under "Template search brief".

## 1. Start from the job, not the trade

A template for "real estate" may be wrong for this realtor, and a hotel template may be right. Write down the shape of the site first, from the market read:

- What the first screen has to do (market read §6).
- What the client can fill it with: a lot of photos or few, video or none, a face or no face (`media-inventory.md`).
- What the page needs to hold: a menu, a product grid, listings, a gallery, plans with what's in each, a map, a booking or request form.
- The feel the direction calls for, in plain words.

## 2. Find five to eight references

Sites that do this job well, for this trade or a neighboring one. Where to look:

- **Land-book** (land-book.com): filters below. Strong for landing pages and for Framer and Webflow templates.
- **Awwwards** (awwwards.com/websites): filter by Category, Tag, Technology, Country, Font, Color. Categories: Architecture, Art & Illustration, Business & Corporate, Culture & Education, Design Agencies, E-Commerce, Events, Experimental, Fashion, Film & TV, Food & Drink, Games & Entertainment, Hotel / Restaurant, Institutions, Luxury, Magazine / Newspaper / Blog, Mobile & Apps, Music & Sound, Other, Photography, Promotional, Real Estate, Social responsibility, Sports, Startups, Technology, Web & Interactive. A category or tag is one path segment: `awwwards.com/websites/real-estate/`.
- **Lapa Ninja** (lapa.ninja): landing pages by category.
- Godly, Siteinspire, One Page Love and Httpster for a wider look.
- The best real businesses in the trade, found in the market read.

For each reference record the link, the one quality worth taking, and what doesn't transfer. A real company's site is studied, never cloned (`SKILL.md` section 2).

## 3. The template search brief

Six searches the builder can run: three close, three wide.

| # | Where | Filters or search words | Why | A good hit looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Close:** the trade's own filter, narrowed by what the site is.

**Wide:** drop the trade and keep the shape. Search by what the page holds and how it should feel. This is where the best templates for small trades are found, because few are labeled for them.

**Neighbors:** trades whose sites have the same shape.

| The client | Same shape as |
|---|---|
| Realtor, home builder | Architecture, Furniture & Interiors, Travel (hotel sites), Photography |
| Plant seller, nursery, florist | Furniture & Interiors, Food & Drinks, Nature, Ecommerce |
| Restaurant, bar, food truck | Food & Drinks, Event, Travel |
| Barber, salon, spa | Beauty, Fashion, Health & Fitness |
| Contractor, auto shop | Architecture, Automotive, Local Business |
| Content or creative agency | Advertising, Marketing, Design, Photography, Movie |
| Gym, coach | Health & Fitness, Sport, Education |
| Law, tax, insurance | Finance, Local Business, Medical (for trust-first layouts) |

### Land-book filters (read from the site 2026-11-12)

- **Sections:** Websites, Sections, Mobile, Motion, OG Images, Headlines. Templates has its own tab, split into Framer and Webflow, with kinds Blog, Landing Page, Other, Portfolio, Store.
- **Page kind:** Landing, Portfolio, Blog, Ecommerce, Other, About Us, Career, Sign Up, Pricing, Case Study, Blog Post, Product listing, Product page.
- **Sort:** Most Recent, Recommended, Most liked, Most viewed.
- **Industry:** Advertising, AI, Animals, Architecture, Art, Automotive, Beauty, Charity, Design, Ecommerce, Education, Event, Fashion, Finance, Food & Drinks, Furniture & Interiors, Gaming, Health & Fitness, HR, Kids, Local Business, Magazine, Marketing, Medical, Movie, Music, Nature, News, NFT / Crypto / Web3, Photography, PR, Productivity, Real Estate, Sport, Tech, Travel, Writing.
- **Style:** 3D, Animation, Apple Style, Background Image, Background Video, Big Footer, Big Type, Big Type in Footer, Black & White, Bottom Navigation, Bright Colors, Brutalism, Cards, Dark Colors, Flat Design, Fun, Gradient, Illustration, Light Colors, Parallax, Pastel Colors, Pattern, People, Stripe Style, Visible Borders.
- **Type:** Agency, Beta, Community, eBook, Infographic, Mobile App, OS App, Personal, Physical Product, Resource, Saas, Service, Single Page, Software.
- **Typography:** Display, Sans Serif, Serif.
- **Color:** Red, Yellow, Green, Azure, Blue, Black, White.
- **Platform:** Figma, Framer, Shopify, Webflow, WordPress.

Filter names change. When one doesn't match, read the filter panel again and update this list. Land-book blocks automated readers, so open it in the builder's browser.

### From the shape to the filters

| What the site has or needs | Land-book filters that find it |
|---|---|
| Strong video | Style: Background Video |
| Strong photos | Style: Background Image |
| Few photos, a clear offer | Style: Big Type; Typography: Display or Serif |
| The owner or the team leads | Style: People; Type: Personal |
| A menu, plans, listings or products | Style: Cards; Page kind: Pricing, Product listing or Ecommerce |
| A calm, simple feel | Style: Light Colors, Black & White, Visible Borders |
| A loud, night-out feel | Style: Dark Colors, Bright Colors, Animation |
| One page, one action | Type: Single Page; Page kind: Landing |
| A service business | Type: Service or Agency; Industry: Local Business |

A realtor with good listing photos and one agent: close is Industry: Real Estate with Page kind: Landing. Wide is Style: Background Image and People with Type: Personal, any industry. Neighbors are Architecture and Furniture & Interiors.

### Other sources

- **Envato and MotionSites:** search words, not filters. Use the shape words ("listing grid", "video hero", "one page", "booking") with the trade word and with each neighbor trade.
- **Framer and Webflow marketplaces:** the same shape words; Land-book's Templates tab covers many of both.

## 4. Note what each source costs

Next to every candidate: subscription the builder has (free to try), paid per template (bought when it's the one), or a real company (study only). `clone-intake` still vets the chosen template against `creative-direction.md` before Phase A.
