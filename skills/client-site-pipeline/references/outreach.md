# Outreach: getting the pitch in front of the owner

The pipeline ends with a live pitch and a folder of deliverables. This is how they reach an owner who never asked for them. The agent drafts; **the builder sends every message from their own account.** The agent never sends, posts or calls.

## The first message

Short enough to read on a lock screen, and about them. Write it from the market read and the language bank, through `copywriting` and `antislop-copywriting`.

1. **One true thing about their business,** specific enough that it couldn't be sent to anyone else: something on their page, a review, a dish, a job they posted.
2. **What was made.** "I built a website for [business]." Say it plainly. It is the hook.
3. **The picture.** One mockup or the coming-soon card (`deliverables/mockups/`, `deliverables/hype/`). Send a single image.
4. **The link** to the live pitch.
5. **One easy question.** "Want me to send over what it would take to put it live?"

Leave out the price, the builder's history, a list of services and anything that reads like a template. Leave out any claim about what the site will do for their sales.

Write it in the language the owner posts in. Give two versions: one for a DM and one to say out loud when walking in, since many owners are easier to reach in person than online.

## Where to send it

The channel the business answers on, seen in the market read: the Instagram or Facebook page they reply from, the email on their listing, or the counter. Use only contact details the business publishes for customers. If they have asked not to be contacted, or the platform's rules forbid unsolicited messages there, don't.

## Following up

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | The first message with one image and the link |
| Day 3 | One line and a different image: a stat card, or the site on a phone |
| Day 7 | The plan poster (`deliverables/pitch/`), and an offer to stop by |
| Day 14 | A last short note: the site stays up for them until a named date |

Four touches, then stop. An owner who says no gets a thank-you and is never messaged again. An owner who replies with anything, a question, a doubt, a competitor's link, goes to feedback diagnosis (`brand-discovery`) before the next message, and the owner sheet is the easy next step: "If it's not quite you yet, this takes five minutes and I'll fix it."

## The pitch tracker

One private file for every pitch, at the path in the builder profile (`~/.client-site-pipeline/pitches.md`). The agent adds a row when a pitch goes live and updates it when the builder says what happened.

| Business | City | Pitch link | First sent (date, channel) | Touches | Last reply, in their words | State | Next step and date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

States: `built`, `sent`, `replied`, `meeting`, `won`, `no`, `quiet`. A `quiet` pitch past its last touch keeps its link alive until the named date, then the showcase deploy comes down or is reused.

What owners say back is research. Add their words to `copy-bank` (the trade's file, and `banned.md` when a line put them off), so the next first message is better than this one.

## Done when

- [ ] Two drafts of the first message, each with one true detail about this business
- [ ] One image chosen and the link checked on a phone
- [ ] The follow-ups drafted, four touches at most
- [ ] The tracker row added
- [ ] The builder has the drafts and knows nothing was sent
