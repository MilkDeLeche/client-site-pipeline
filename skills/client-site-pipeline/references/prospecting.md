# Prospecting: finding a business worth pitching

Optional, and it runs before step 0. The builder gives a place; the agent brings back a short list of local businesses that would gain the most from a site, with the reason for each. The builder picks one and the pipeline starts as usual.

Cast wide. The builder pitches many owners and expects most not to answer, so the list favors businesses that are easy to help over ones that look like sure things.

## 1. Ask where

A city, a neighborhood or a stretch of road, and optionally the trades to look at. With no trades given, use the ones the pipeline has built for and the ones that live on photos: food and drink, beauty and barber, home services, auto, fitness, retail, real estate, events.

## 2. Look the way a customer would

Search Google Maps for each trade in that place and open the listings. Google Maps and Instagram block automated readers: use the builder's browser, and stop at any login wall or challenge. For each business note what is on the public listing:

| Signal | Where it shows | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| No website on the listing | The listing has no site button | The clearest opening |
| The "website" is a Facebook, Instagram or link-in-bio page | The site button | They want one and settled |
| A site that fails on a phone, is unfinished, or is years out of date | Open it at phone width | A before-and-after is easy to show |
| Busy and well reviewed | Review count and how recent | They have customers and something to show |
| Active on Instagram or Facebook | Recent posts | Real photos exist to build from |
| English only, in a bilingual area | Their site and posts | A second language is a visible gain |
| No way to ask, book or order online | Their site | The request form is the pitch |

## 3. Score honestly

A good prospect has an opening (the first three rows) and proof they are alive (the next two). Skip a business that:

- is a chain or franchise location, since the owner can't decide;
- already has a good, current site;
- is closed, or has had no reviews and no posts for a year;
- is in a trade whose advertising the builder can't do properly (regulated medical or legal claims) unless the builder asks for it.

## 4. Bring back ten or fewer

`prospects.md` in the builder's private folder (`~/.client-site-pipeline/prospects/<place>-<date>.md`), never in a client repo:

| Business | Trade | Listing link | What they have now | The opening | Proof they're alive | What we could show them | Read on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

"What we could show them" is one line: the first screen their site could have, from what is already on their page. Record only what is public on the listing. No personal phone numbers or emails gathered from elsewhere, and no guesses written as facts.

## 5. Hand off

The builder picks. That row becomes the kickoff brief (`kickoff.md` section 3), and the pick goes into the pitch tracker (`outreach.md`) with the date.
