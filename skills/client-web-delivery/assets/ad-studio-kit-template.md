## Every week

1. Ten minutes of trend checking: Pinterest, Reddit, and Meta's Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) for what businesses like this one are running now.
2. Pick five lines from the niche hooks (`copy-bank` → the trade's file, "Ad hooks by niche"), one per niche or service, none used in the last four weeks.
3. For each ad: pick the product, the shape (9:16 for Reels and Stories, 4:5 for the feed), and paste into the instructions box:
   > Headline, exactly: "{{line}}". Small line above it: "{{who it's for}}". Make it look like a native Instagram post: the real photo big, the headline in bold type. No prices, no numbers, no reviews or star ratings, no people's faces. The only other words allowed are "{{brand}}" and "{{call to action}}".
4. Read every word on every ad. Anything invented (a number, "best in town", a review, a face, another company's logo) fails.
5. Save the passed ones to `deliverables/ads/<year>-w<week>/` and add the week to `ads-log.md` with the next due date.
