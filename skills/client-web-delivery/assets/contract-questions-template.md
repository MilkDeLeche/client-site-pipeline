## What's not included

Say these up front. It builds trust.

- **The domain renewal.** They pay their registrar directly, usually about $20 a year.
- **Paid ads.** Google or Facebook ad spend is separate.
- **Photography.** We use the photos they already have or send us.
- **Guaranteed rankings.** We set up Google, Maps and AI search correctly. Nobody honest can promise a #1 spot.

**Can you guarantee more sales?** No. The site makes it easy for customers to reach them{{ in both languages}}, and every request is saved. Closing the sale is still their part.
