# Banned lines

Never shown to an owner. A line in here can't be fixed by swapping a word. Go back to the buyer's words and a pattern instead.

## 3. AI tells

The structures `antislop-copywriting` flags, here so the list is in one place: "It's not X, it's Y" and "X is not Y" reveals; "No X. No Y. Just Z."; three-beat lists used for rhythm; staccato fragments as a stunt ("Real spots. Real footage."); em dashes; sentences that trail into extra comma clauses; a heading phrased as an instruction.
