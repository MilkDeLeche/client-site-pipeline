# Measurement and reporting

## Set up before you start

Without a baseline you cannot prove the work did anything. Capture it in Phase 00, before making a single change.

- **Google Search Console** — verified, sitemap submitted. The only free source of real query data, and the single most important account.
- **Google Analytics 4** — organic traffic, conversions defined as something that matters (calls, direction requests, orders), not "pageviews".
- **Google Business Profile Insights** — for a local business.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools** — free, two minutes, feeds Copilot.
- **A local rank grid** — for local clients, ranking-by-distance, not one number.
- **The AI query log** — the manual query set from `geo-aeo.md`.

Export and save the Phase 00 state. Search Console only retains 16 months, and the comparison you want in a year won't be available then.

## What to report

**Business outcomes**, first and largest:
- Calls, direction requests, form submissions, orders
- Revenue attributable to organic where it can be tracked

**Search performance:**
- Organic clicks and impressions, vs previous period *and* vs the same period last year — seasonality is real and year-over-year is the honest comparison
- Average position for the tracked keyword set
- Keywords entering the top 3 / top 10
- Pages ranking 11–30 — the pipeline
- Click-through rate against position

**Local:**
- Discovery vs direct searches — discovery growth means new customers finding the business, which is the whole point
- Calls, direction requests, website clicks from GBP
- Review count and average, and the rate of new reviews
- Local pack position across the service area, not just at the pin

**AI visibility:**
- The query set re-run, same wording, monthly
- Cited / not cited, and who was cited instead
- Accuracy of what was said

**Technical health:**
- Core Web Vitals passing rate from field data
- Indexed pages vs submitted
- Crawl errors, new 404s

## Reporting honestly

**Vanity metrics to avoid.** "Impressions up 400%" usually means the site started surfacing for irrelevant queries. "Keywords ranking: 1,200" counts position 97. Report what changed for the business.

**Attribution is imperfect.** Organic, direct and branded search bleed into each other; a customer who saw an Instagram post and then googled the name shows up as organic. Say so rather than claiming credit for everything.

**Report losses.** A ranking that dropped, a page that lost traffic, a fix that didn't work. A report with only good news isn't believed twice.

**Separate what you did from what happened.** Google runs core updates on its own schedule. Sometimes traffic moves for reasons unrelated to the work. Note the update dates on the timeline.

## Expectations

Say this at the start, not when asked why it isn't working:

| Work | First movement | Meaningful |
|---|---|---|
| Technical fixes | 1–4 weeks | 1–2 months |
| GBP optimization | Days | 2–4 weeks |
| Reviews | Weeks | Ongoing, compounding |
| New content | 2–3 months | 6–12 months |
| Link building | 3–6 months | 12+ months |
| AI citation | Months | Unpredictable |

New domains take longer than established ones. Competitive categories take longer than quiet ones. Nobody can promise a position by a date, and anyone who does is either guessing or lying.
